Hey Spelunkr folks 👋

Big fan. I made a little TV app so I could watch Spelunkr on my LG TV (and a Roku version) with nothing but the remote: up/down flips channels, OK opens a Prevue-style guide, left/right shows what's on and what's next. It uses your fonts, colors and logos, and plays the same lineup, bumps and "up next" cards in sync with the site. No ads, no accounts, no changes to the programming. Every tape still plays from the Internet Archive.

It's a personal sideload and nobody else has it. It worked by reading your public lineup/manifest JSON from data.spelunkr.tv. Since the recent update those requests get a Cloudflare 403 unless they come from spelunkr.tv itself, so the TV app stopped working. For now I've switched the TV back to loading spelunkr.tv directly, which works fine, just with the busier desktop interface.

I don't want to get around the block, so I'm asking: would you be up for allowing a TV app like this (an allowlisted origin, a key, whatever suits you)? Or, if you'd rather do official LG/Roku apps, I'm happy to hand over the code. It's small, and the Roku version is checked against your schedule engine so the lineup matches the site exactly.

Either way, thanks for building the weirdest cable company on the internet. Technical details below 👇
